# Maze prototype

A first-person maze, step by step, to see whether that kind of view fits the GT 6 Pro. It is its own app, `apps/maze/` (bundle `com.hectorreto.maze`, name "Maze" on the watch), with the maze on `pages/index/`. It is an original game, not a port of an existing one. Before the apps were split it lived on the branch `laberinto`, with the game moved to `pages/game/` and a long press to open it; that link is gone now that the game is its own app.

## How it works

- **Maze:** square, carved by a depth-first search from the top-left cell with a small seeded random generator (seeded from the clock). The exit is a door in the outer wall next to the bottom-right cell, drawn green. Reaching it shows the result screen (see **Stars**); a tap there makes the next level.
- **Levels and record:** the side grows by level: 7, 9, 11, 13, then 15 cells (grid 2 x cells + 1). `@system.storage` keeps `level` and the fewest steps per size as `best<cells>`; a new maze starts by showing "Récord: N pasos" and the line under the compass shows the level and the gems.
- **Stars:** each maze scores up to three: one for leaving, one for all its gems, one for leaving within par. Par is 1.5 x the shortest walk through every gem and out; the maze is a tree, so `shortestWalk` gets it from the carving's `parent` array (twice the cells on the way to any target, minus the way to the exit, plus the door), and that array is dropped after `makeMaze`. In Node, blind depth-first walks taking all gems get three stars 43% of the time on level 1, 33% on level 2 and 9% on level 5, guardian included (a person sees dead ends from afar, so should do better). The result screen is a black `stack` with an `if`, with fixed `image`s and `text`s (no `for`), and shows the stars, gems, steps against par, the record, and the total with its rank (`RANKS`) and what the next rank needs. It ignores taps for 0.6 s, since the player is usually tapping fast when the exit comes. `stars` in storage keeps the total; saves from before it start at 0, and `level` and `best<cells>` are unchanged. `common/star.png` and `common/star_off.png` are 64 px RGBA, under 0.5 KB each.
- **Menu and daily challenge:** the app opens on a menu: the top half continues the levels, the bottom half opens the challenge, and it shows the level, the streak with today's stars, and the total and rank. No canvas lives under it, so it starts without one, and it is one more `if` `stack` of a few bindings. The challenge is a maze made from the date (`today()` seeds the generator, with the first 8 numbers dropped since nearby seeds start alike), the same all day, of level 2's size with its slow guardian, and it does not move the level. Its first finish each day extends the streak and pays a bonus of one star per day of streak, up to 5. A streak is lost once a whole day passes without a finish. Replays only add the stars they beat that day's best by, so it cannot be farmed. `daily` in storage is one string, `date,streak,stars,best`, so finishing costs two writes (with `stars`). Saves from before it start with no streak.
- **Resuming:** closing the app with the crown no longer loses the maze. `run` in storage holds the seed, the kind of maze, the level, the position, facing, steps, guardian steps and a bit per gem taken: nine numbers, some 40 characters. It is written 1 s after the last tap, only if something changed, and in `onDestroy`, the same timing the clicker uses on this watch, so a run of fast taps writes nothing. `win` clears it. On opening, the menu says "en curso" on that row and tapping it remakes the maze from the seed and puts the player back; the other row starts its own maze, and the first save overwrites the old run. A run is dropped if its level is not the saved level (a lost write) or if it is a challenge from another day. **The map is not saved**, only rebuilt from the way from the start, so side trips disappear from it: the SDK does not say how long a value the watch holds, and the walked cells of a large maze would take hundreds of characters, so this was left out rather than risked.
- **Floor and ceiling:** each new canvas first gets two rects, ceiling and floor; a column that shrinks is painted over with those colours instead of black.
- **Themes:** `THEMES` gives each level, in turn, its wall, ceiling and floor colours (level modulo 5), so mazes keep looking new after the size stops growing at level 5. No green walls (the exit door is green) and no cyan (the gems are). It costs no extra rects.
- **View:** one ray per column, 39 columns of 12 px, cast from the centre of the player's cell with a grid DDA (field of view about 66 degrees). Wall height is 380 / distance px. Colour: x and y faces differ (0.75 brightness on y faces) and walls darken in four distance bands, so a column keeps its colour over small changes. The colour strings are kept per maze (at most 16), so a tap does not rebuild 39 of them.
- **Drawing:** only what changed (as `deltaWalls` in the drawing lab): when a column keeps its colour, only the top and bottom bands it grew or lost; when the colour changes, the whole new wall plus the bands it lost in the ceiling and floor colours. Up to 900 draw calls per canvas, then the canvas is remade and the view drawn in full (a lite canvas takes about 1,100 `fillRect` and has no `clearRect`). In Node, random walks cost about 35-44 rects per action (turns change nearly every column), so the canvas is remade about every 20 actions.
- **Map:** a tap on the compass (y < 100, between the turn zones) remakes the canvas with a map of the grid cells walked so far (one rect per horizontal run, about 110 rects in Node walks, at most about half the grid) and the player as a yellow cell with a mark on the side it faces; any tap remakes the canvas with the view.
- **Gems:** three gems per maze in random dead ends, picked up by stepping on them ("Gema N de 3"). They are `image`s of `common/gem.png` (64 px RGBA, made by `tools/make_gem.js` with Node's `zlib`) in a `for` over the canvas, each placed and sized with a bound `style` (left, top, width, height): 0.35 of the wall height at that distance, a little below the horizon. A gem shows only when the wall in its middle column is farther than it (no partial hiding behind corners). Far gems come first so near ones cover them. The `for` is only set when there is a gem to show or hide, not on every draw: swapping a `for` array is what closed the lab on the watch after many changes (see `compare-drawing-methods.md`).
- **Guardian:** from level 2 on (`GUARD_FROM_LEVEL`, so old saves past level 1 meet it at once) a guardian wakes at the start after 3 steps per cell of the side and follows the player, one cell every 3 of the player's steps, every 2 from level 4 on. It moves only when the player steps (no timers), and catching the player sends them back along the way by 2 grid cells per cell of the side (18 to 30), with steps, gems and map kept and the guardian asleep at the start again, so it costs distance and usually the par star, not the maze. Sending them to the start was the first version: in Node, blind players on the largest mazes then had to walk some 180 cells again per catch, some 180 taps on the watch. It walks only `trail`, the way from the start to the player (a stack: the maze is a tree, so a step back pops it), and its place is computed from `guardSteps` alone. **It must not hunt from anywhere else:** a tree has one path to the exit, so a chaser that started ahead of the player, or took any path but this one, would block it for good; on the trail it is always behind the player, and walking straight to the exit always wins. It is one fixed `image` of `common/guardian.png` (64 px RGBA, under 0.5 KB), resized like the gems, with its feet on the floor and moved off the screen when not in view, not a `for` or an `if`, so no views are made as it comes and goes. The map shows it as a red cell (one rect more). The note warns "Guardián a N pasos" within 6 cells and "El guardián despertó". The trail adds at most some 450 numbers to the heap at 15 x 15 cells; nothing else grows. In Node, blind depth-first walks heading only for the exit are caught at least once in 9% of runs on level 2 and 45% on level 5; those taking all gems in 83% and 97%, 2 to 4 catches a run. A straight walk to the exit was never caught.
- **Vibration:** `@system.vibrator` (it worked on this watch in the clicker, later removed there; see `create-and-preview-the-app.md`), for rare events only: short for a gem and for each tap's walk while the guardian is within 3 cells, long for a catch and for leaving a maze. Never on turns, walls or plain steps. `config.json` declares `ohos.permission.VIBRATE` with `reason` and `usedScene`, which the watch's installer requires (see `install-on-the-watch.md`). To remove it, drop that entry and the `buzz` calls.
- **Controls:** a full-screen `div` on top takes the taps: the left third (x < 155) turns left, the right third turns right, the bottom (y > 340) turns around, the compass opens the map, the rest of the middle walks one maze cell forward ("Pared" if there is a wall). That is two grid cells, since the one between two maze cells offers no choice; it halves the taps (a 15 x 15 maze needed some 180 just for the way to the exit). Steps still count grid cells, so old records and par compare, the guardian and gems are checked on each, and only the end is drawn. In Node a tap still costs about 54 rects and a canvas remake still comes about every 16 taps, with some 40% fewer rects per grid cell walked. The top shows the compass (N, E, S, O), the steps and the par. The screen is kept on with `brightness.setKeepScreenOn` until 2 minutes go by without a tap (one timer, reset by each tap, the same one that saves the maze); then the system's timeout applies, which closes the app, and the maze resumes on opening. Before this a watch left alone with the app open stayed lit indefinitely.
- **Taps need the click's own position:** `ontouchstart` on that `div` (with only `onclick` and `onlongpress` besides) never fired in the Previewer, so the zone read 233,233 every time and every tap stepped forward; the `click` event itself has `globalX` and `globalY`, so `tap(e)` reads them.

## Status

Checked in the Previewer (October 2026, on the branch): the view draws, stepping, both turns, turning around and "Pared" work. On the GT 6 Pro (October 2026, on the branch) it installed over the previous app and everything works: it looks very good, the tap zones respond well, the hitch when the canvas is remade was not noticeable, and reaching the exit makes a new maze. As its own app (October 2026) it builds signed with its own debug profile (see `add-a-new-app.md`) and installs on the watch next to the other apps. Levels, record, floor and ceiling, map and gems (branch `laberinto-mejoras`, October 2026) were checked in Node (random walks with stubbed canvas and storage: levels advance, the map stays near 110 rects, every gem straight ahead is shown and centred) and in the Previewer (floor and ceiling, the level line, the map with the walked path and facing mark, a gem two cells ahead growing at one cell and "Gema 1 de N" when stepped on). On the GT 6 Pro (November 2026) they look and work very well, so a bound `width`/`height` does resize an `image` on the watch, and the map and the extra canvas remakes feel fine. Themes, stars, the guardian, walking by whole cells, the daily challenge, vibration and resuming (November 2026) were checked only in Node, through the page's own code with stubbed canvas and storage: not yet in the Previewer or on the watch. To check there: that the screen goes off about 2 minutes after the last tap and the maze then resumes, that closing with the crown and reopening resumes the maze, that the app installs with the permission on its own debug profile, whether the vibration feels right, the layouts of the menu and the result screen, that `new Date()` gives the local date on the watch (the challenge and streak depend on it), whether walking two grid cells per tap is easy to follow, the wider top line, how the guardian image looks and how fast it resizes up to 323 px when one cell away, and whether it is hidden when off the screen.
