# Maze prototype

A first-person maze, step by step, to see whether that kind of view fits the GT 6 Pro. It is its own app, `apps/maze/` (bundle `com.hectorreto.maze`, name "Maze" on the watch), with the maze on `pages/index/`. It is an original game, not a port of an existing one. Before the apps were split it lived on the branch `laberinto`, with the game moved to `pages/game/` and a long press to open it; that link is gone now that the game is its own app.

## How it works

- **Maze:** square, carved by a depth-first search from the top-left cell with a small seeded random generator (seeded from the clock). The exit is a door in the outer wall next to the bottom-right cell, drawn green. Reaching it shows "Salida en N pasos" (or "Nuevo récord") for 1.5 s and makes the next level.
- **Levels and record:** the side grows by level: 7, 9, 11, 13, then 15 cells (grid 2 x cells + 1). `@system.storage` keeps `level` and the fewest steps per size as `best<cells>`; a new maze starts by showing "Récord: N pasos" and the line under the compass shows the level and the gems.
- **Floor and ceiling:** each new canvas first gets two rects, a dark blue ceiling and a brown floor; a column that shrinks is painted over with those colours instead of black.
- **View:** one ray per column, 39 columns of 12 px, cast from the centre of the player's cell with a grid DDA (field of view about 66 degrees). Wall height is 380 / distance px. Colour: x and y faces differ (0.75 brightness on y faces) and walls darken in four distance bands, so a column keeps its colour over small changes.
- **Drawing:** only what changed (as `deltaWalls` in the drawing lab): when a column keeps its colour, only the top and bottom bands it grew or lost; when the colour changes, the whole new wall plus the bands it lost in the ceiling and floor colours. Up to 900 draw calls per canvas, then the canvas is remade and the view drawn in full (a lite canvas takes about 1,100 `fillRect` and has no `clearRect`). In Node, random walks cost about 35-44 rects per action (turns change nearly every column), so the canvas is remade about every 20 actions.
- **Map:** a tap on the compass (y < 100, between the turn zones) remakes the canvas with a map of the grid cells walked so far (one rect per horizontal run, about 110 rects in Node walks, at most about half the grid) and the player as a yellow cell with a mark on the side it faces; any tap remakes the canvas with the view.
- **Gems:** three gems per maze in random dead ends, picked up by stepping on them ("Gema N de 3"). They are `image`s of `common/gem.png` (64 px RGBA, made by `tools/make_gem.js` with Node's `zlib`) in a `for` over the canvas, each placed and sized with a bound `style` (left, top, width, height): 0.35 of the wall height at that distance, a little below the horizon. A gem shows only when the wall in its middle column is farther than it (no partial hiding behind corners). Far gems come first so near ones cover them.
- **Controls:** a full-screen `div` on top takes the taps: the left third (x < 155) turns left, the right third turns right, the bottom (y > 340) turns around, the compass opens the map, the rest of the middle steps forward ("Pared" if there is a wall). The top shows the compass (N, E, S, O) and the steps. The screen is kept on with `brightness.setKeepScreenOn` while the maze is open.
- **Taps need the click's own position:** `ontouchstart` on that `div` (with only `onclick` and `onlongpress` besides) never fired in the Previewer, so the zone read 233,233 every time and every tap stepped forward; the `click` event itself has `globalX` and `globalY`, so `tap(e)` reads them.

## Status

Checked in the Previewer (October 2026, on the branch): the view draws, stepping, both turns, turning around and "Pared" work. On the GT 6 Pro (October 2026, on the branch) it installed over the previous app and everything works: it looks very good, the tap zones respond well, the hitch when the canvas is remade was not noticeable, and reaching the exit makes a new maze. As its own app (October 2026) it builds signed with its own debug profile (see `add-a-new-app.md`) and installs on the watch next to the other apps. Levels, record, floor and ceiling, map and gems (branch `laberinto-mejoras`, October 2026) were checked in Node (random walks with stubbed canvas and storage: levels advance, the map stays near 110 rects, every gem straight ahead is shown and centred) and in the Previewer (floor and ceiling, the level line, the map with the walked path and facing mark, a gem two cells ahead growing at one cell and "Gema 1 de N" when stepped on); not yet on the watch. Unknown on the watch: whether a bound `width`/`height` resizes an `image` (the watch scales images with smooth filtering, see `compare-drawing-methods.md`), and whether the map look and the extra canvas remakes feel right.
