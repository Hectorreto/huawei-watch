# Brotín

An original virtual pet: a plant creature in a pot, with its own art and rules, not a port of an existing game. It is `apps/brotin/` (bundle `com.hectorreto.brotin`, name "Brotín" on the watch). The text is Spanish only.

## How it works

- **Rules:** `pages/index/pet.js` holds all of them, with no views or watch APIs, so they run in Node; `index.js` only draws and handles taps. Water, sun and mood fall with real time, also while the app is closed: `advance` works out the time since `last` on opening and every 30 s, in at most 200 steps since the watch's JS is slow. Bugs appear by day, about one per 3 hours, at most 3.
- **Night (22:00-7:00, local):** the pet sleeps. Needs fall at a quarter of the speed, sun does not fall and no bugs appear. Sun, play and stroking are refused.
- **Health:** any need at 0 or 3 bugs wear it down, in 24 hours however many troubles there are; it recovers when all needs are above 40, and below 50 health the note says so, or that it is recovering: the sick face alone did not tell what to do, since needs at 25-40 show no other hint and yet stop the healing. At 0 the pet leaves a new seed and the generation goes up, so nothing is lost for good. It used to take 12 hours, but in a Node simulation of whole lives even a player filling everything 3 times a day mostly lost the plant before it matured, and neglect always killed it, so no neglected adult could exist. Each trouble also used to add to the wear, so with all needs at 0 a plant left full at 21:00 was gone in 22 hours, one missed day; now it lasts 37 hours (33 from half bars).
- **Growth:** seed, sprout at 30 min, young at 12 h, adult at 48 h. The adult depends on the average of needs and health over its life, plus 3 per wish granted: golden flower from 87, a flower from 70, cactus from 50, mushroom below. The flower depends on what the player gave: sunflower with more suns than waterings, lily with at least twice the waterings of suns, otherwise the pink one.
- **Seed and herbarium:** the adult used to be the end of the game. A day after maturing (72 h of age) it has a seed, shown left of the pot; two taps on it plant a new seed, with the second tap needed while the question shows, because the adult is replaced. Nothing forces it: the adult stays until planted or withered. Generation, plants, record, streak, today's wish and `found` (a bit per adult form, in `ADULTS` order) carry over; a save that already holds an adult marks it as found on loading. The status view leads to the herbarium with a tap: 6 fixed images, `mini_locked.png` until found, and the clue to the first one missing. Their `src` is only set on opening it. A tap on an image shows its name, or its clue if it is missing, so a player after one form need not find the others first; the same image again or outside the grid closes the view. `herbariumPlace` works out the grid from the heights in `index.css`, since the column is centred: change one and the other follows.
- **What each adult takes (simulated, 300 lives each; a visit fills what is named, strokes to the cap and plays a game of 6 sparks if the mood is under 80):** 3-6 visits a day filling everything gives the pink flower, and with strokes only it takes 4 or more; 2 visits, a cactus; 3-4 visits giving 2 waterings, 1 sun and strokes to 50, a mushroom; filling the water and giving sun only below 45 on 5-6 visits, a lily; filling the sun with 1 watering on 8 visits, a sunflower; filling everything on 8 visits, a golden flower most times. One visit a day withers it, even filling everything, since the water lasts 6 hours. The sunflower is the most demanding. Those two are the first things to loosen if they prove too hard in play.
- **Pots:** earned for good and kept across generations, they give the streak, the record and the herbarium something to lead to: blue for a best streak of 3 days, pink for 15 sparks in one game, gold for the full herbarium. They are worked out from `bestStreak`, `best` and `found`, so no pot needs saving apart from the one in use (`pot`) and how many were told (`potsSeen`). With more than one earned a tap on the pot changes it; with only one it strokes the pet as before. The herbarium shows what the next one takes. Each costs about 32 KB in the package.
- **Wish of the day:** each local day (`rollDay`, on opening and every 30 s) the pet asks for 2 waterings, 2 suns, 5 strokes or 6 sparks, never the same twice in a row; the note shows the progress when nothing is urgent. Granting it adds 15 mood, 3 points to the care average of a pet that reaches adulthood, and a day to the streak in the status view; a local day with none granted ends the streak. Withering keeps the streak. Cleaning is not a wish, since it needs a bug to have appeared. The day comes from `dayNumber`, plain arithmetic over `getFullYear`, `getMonth` and `getDate`, because `Date.UTC` has not been checked on the watch.
- **Taps:** one full-screen `div` takes every tap and `hit` in `pet.js` says what it means, as in the maze. Bottom buttons: water, sun, play, clean. The bars at the top open the status view, a tap there opens the herbarium and another returns; a tap on the pet strokes it. A stroke adds 3 mood only up to 60, so above that the game is what helps; when it adds nothing the note says "Quiere jugar", unless the mood is already 90 or a stroke wish is pending, whose progress that would hide. Unlimited free mood made the game pointless. Strokes do not write to storage each time, unless one grants the wish.
- **Game:** sparks appear inside the round screen until 3 get away. Each stays 1.1 s, 40 ms less per spark caught, down to 650 ms (`sparkMs`), one timer per spark as before, so a long game adds no timers or drawing. Each one caught adds 6 mood, and the best game is saved as `best` and shown in the status view. It used to be a fixed 8 sparks, which left no score to beat. After the first 3 caught, 12 % of what appears is a golden spark worth 3, and 15 % a bug that costs a life if tapped and nothing if left to go. The kind is one bound `src`, so a plain game only swaps the image when the kind changes.
- **Drawing:** no `for`, `if` or canvas, since making views closed the lab on the watch and froze the Previewer (`compare-drawing-methods.md`). All views exist from the start and switch with `show`. The body is one image whose bound `top` moves 6 px each second for the breathing, behind the pot's rim; the face is a separate image over it, so 9 forms and 6 faces need no image per combination. The sky colour is bound.
- **Art:** `tools/make_brotin_sprites.js` writes every PNG and the launcher icon from text grids in the script, scaled up with nearest neighbour because the watch blurs what it scales. With a second argument it writes a contact sheet to check the art without building.
- **Do not name a page method `render`:** the compiled page has its own, so calling it threw `Expected a function` and the page stayed on its initial data, with no error but a bare `JSI:CallFunction error returned!` in `previewer.log`. A temporary `try`/`catch` showing `e.message` in a text found it. The method is `refresh`.
- **Do not call `clearTimeout` on a timer from inside its own callback:** in the Previewer the game stopped after its first spark when `hideSpark`, called by that spark's timer, cleared it and then set the next one; taking the clear out of that path fixed it. Only a tap, which comes while the timer is pending, clears it now.
- **Visitors:** by day, at most one roll per 2 hours (`rollVisitor`, on opening and every 30 s), with a 60 % chance, something comes by up and to the right of the pet and stays until tapped, also across closing the app. A butterfly adds 25 mood, past the strokes' cap; a cloud adds 25 water; a ladybug eats all the bugs and adds 10 mood; 1 time in 10 a shooting star adds 25 mood and grants today's wish. It gives a reason to open the app that the wish alone does not: something different may be waiting. It costs one image, moved 6 px per second only while there is a visitor.
- **Reactions:** each action done shows a 54 px sign over the pet's head for the 1.2 s of its happy face: a heart for a stroke, a drop for water, a sun for sun, a star for cleaning, a game or planting. It is one image whose bound `src` changes, so it only costs an image swap (about 50 ms on the watch, per the lab) when the sign differs from the last, and it shares the happy face's timer.
- **Voice and sky:** after 30 minutes away it greets by the hour, after 12 hours "¡Te extrañé!". When nothing else needs saying the note shows a line from `idleLine`, changing every 30 s with the tick that was already there, among them the streak and the herbarium count. The sky is one bound colour for dawn, day, dusk, evening and night (`skyFor`), so it adds no images.
- **Messages:** `say` takes a list that follows the first message, 2 s each, so a long absence tells everything that happened (the form, "Nueva en el herbario", the seed) instead of only the last event. A refusal goes through `refuse`: the next action done removes it at once, or "No hay bichos" stayed for 2 s after a watering, as if it answered that.
- **Saving:** new fields go at the end of `FIELDS`: an older save lacks them and they load as 0, so old saves keep working. `pet` in `@system.storage` as a comma-separated string, after each action but a plain stroke, on growth, every 10 minutes and on closing. If reading fails the app plays a new pet without saving, since writing would replace a save that may still be there: with a herbarium and pots there is more to lose. A lost write only costs the last actions, since the time is worked out again from the saved `last`.

## Testing in the Previewer

Raise `TIME_SCALE` in `pet.js` to see a whole life quickly (at 2880, an hour of game passes per 1.25 s) and put it back to 1 before building for the watch.

## Status

Not yet seen in the Previewer or on the watch, checked only in Node (rules, old saves, taps, simulated lives): the sparks game with lives, the wish of the day, the seed and the herbarium, the three new forms with their faces, the signs over the head, the visitors, the golden sparks and bugs, the pots, the greetings, the colours of the sky and the taps on the herbarium's images, whose zones come from the layout worked out by hand. The 650 ms spark may be too short on the watch. A run of the real `index.js` in Node, with fake timers, clock and storage, went through every view, old saves, planting, visitors and 6,000 random taps with no error, no missing image and no timer clearing itself. It cannot check the layout. They compile with hvigor from the command line: `LegacyCompileLiteJS` and `LegacyGenerateLiteCode` pass, and so does the signed build. The unsigned `.bin` is 1.97 MB: the build stores images uncompressed, so a 180 px form takes about 130 KB however simple its PNG, and a herbarium image, 60 px, about 14 KB.

The rules were checked in Node (November 2026): saving, night, growth to each adult form, withering to a new seed, the clock going backwards, refusals and tap zones. The app compiles (`LegacyCompileLiteJS` and `LegacyGenerateLiteCode` pass). In the Previewer (November 2026): the main view and its layout, the seed's face in place, the water and clean buttons with their refusals, the status view, stroking, and the game starting, hiding the buttons and ending. Catching a spark, the sun button, night, bugs and the adult forms have not been seen there. It has its own debug profile, `brotin.p7b` (`BrotinDebugProfile`, November 2026), and the signed HAP builds; it has not been installed on the watch yet. What is left to check is in `watch-test-list.md`.
