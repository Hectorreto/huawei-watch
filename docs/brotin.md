# Brotín

An original virtual pet: a plant creature in a pot, with its own art and rules, not a port of an existing game. It is `apps/brotin/` (bundle `com.hectorreto.brotin`, name "Brotín" on the watch). The text is Spanish only.

## How it works

- **Rules:** `pages/index/pet.js` holds all of them, with no views or watch APIs, so they run in Node; `index.js` only draws and handles taps. Water, sun and mood fall with real time, also while the app is closed: `advance` works out the time since `last` on opening and every 30 s, in at most 200 steps since the watch's JS is slow. Bugs appear by day, about one per 3 hours, at most 3.
- **Night (22:00-7:00, local):** the pet sleeps. Needs fall at a quarter of the speed, sun does not fall and no bugs appear. Sun, play and stroking are refused.
- **Health:** a need at 0 or 3 bugs each wear it down (24 hours with one trouble); it recovers when all needs are above 40. At 0 the pet leaves a new seed and the generation goes up, so nothing is lost for good. It used to take 12 hours, but in a Node simulation of whole lives even a player filling everything 3 times a day mostly lost the plant before it matured, and neglect always killed it, so no neglected adult could exist.
- **Growth:** seed, sprout at 30 min, young at 12 h, adult at 48 h. The adult depends on the average of needs and health over its life, plus 3 per wish granted: golden flower from 86, a flower from 70, cactus from 50, mushroom below. The flower depends on what the player gave: sunflower with more suns than waterings, lily with at least twice the waterings of suns, otherwise the pink one.
- **Seed and herbarium:** the adult used to be the end of the game. A day after maturing (72 h of age) it has a seed, shown left of the pot; two taps on it plant a new seed, with the second tap needed while the question shows, because the adult is replaced. Nothing forces it: the adult stays until planted or withered. Generation, plants, record, streak, today's wish and `found` (a bit per adult form, in `ADULTS` order) carry over; a save that already holds an adult marks it as found on loading. The status view leads to the herbarium with a tap: 6 fixed images, `mini_locked.png` until found, and the clue to the first one missing. Their `src` is only set on opening it.
- **What each adult takes (simulated, 300 lives each):** filling everything 3-6 times a day gives the pink flower; 2 times a day, a cactus; 4 visits giving only part of what is missing, a mushroom; filling the water and giving sun only below 45 on 5-6 visits, a lily (about 2 in 3); filling the sun with 1 watering on 8 visits, a sunflower; filling everything on 8 visits with the wishes, a golden flower (about 3 in 4). The sunflower is the most demanding, and a good thing to loosen if it proves too hard in play.
- **Wish of the day:** each local day (`rollDay`, on opening and every 30 s) the pet asks for 2 waterings, 2 suns, 5 strokes or 6 sparks, never the same twice in a row; the note shows the progress when nothing is urgent. Granting it adds 15 mood, 3 points to the care average of a pet that reaches adulthood, and a day to the streak in the status view; a local day with none granted ends the streak. Withering keeps the streak. Cleaning is not a wish, since it needs a bug to have appeared. The day comes from `dayNumber`, plain arithmetic over `getFullYear`, `getMonth` and `getDate`, because `Date.UTC` has not been checked on the watch.
- **Taps:** one full-screen `div` takes every tap and `hit` in `pet.js` says what it means, as in the maze. Bottom buttons: water, sun, play, clean. The bars at the top open the status view, a tap there opens the herbarium and another returns; a tap on the pet strokes it.
- **Game:** sparks appear inside the round screen until 3 get away. Each stays 1.1 s, 40 ms less per spark caught, down to 650 ms (`sparkMs`), one timer per spark as before, so a long game adds no timers or drawing. Each one caught adds 6 mood, and the best game is saved as `best` and shown in the status view. It used to be a fixed 8 sparks, which left no score to beat.
- **Drawing:** no `for`, `if` or canvas, since making views closed the lab on the watch and froze the Previewer (`compare-drawing-methods.md`). All views exist from the start and switch with `show`. The body is one image whose bound `top` moves 6 px each second for the breathing, behind the pot's rim; the face is a separate image over it, so 9 forms and 6 faces need no image per combination. The sky colour is bound.
- **Art:** `tools/make_brotin_sprites.js` writes every PNG and the launcher icon from text grids in the script, scaled up with nearest neighbour because the watch blurs what it scales. With a second argument it writes a contact sheet to check the art without building.
- **Do not name a page method `render`:** the compiled page has its own, so calling it threw `Expected a function` and the page stayed on its initial data, with no error but a bare `JSI:CallFunction error returned!` in `previewer.log`. A temporary `try`/`catch` showing `e.message` in a text found it. The method is `refresh`.
- **Do not call `clearTimeout` on a timer from inside its own callback:** in the Previewer the game stopped after its first spark when `hideSpark`, called by that spark's timer, cleared it and then set the next one; taking the clear out of that path fixed it. Only a tap, which comes while the timer is pending, clears it now.
- **Saving:** new fields go at the end of `FIELDS`: an older save lacks them and they load as 0, so old saves keep working. `pet` in `@system.storage` as a comma-separated string, after each action, on growth, every 10 minutes and on closing. A lost write only costs the last actions, since the time is worked out again from the saved `last`.

## Testing in the Previewer

Raise `TIME_SCALE` in `pet.js` to see a whole life quickly (at 2880, an hour of game passes per 1.25 s) and put it back to 1 before building for the watch.

## Status

Not yet seen in the Previewer or on the watch, checked only in Node (rules, old saves, taps, simulated lives): the sparks game with lives, the wish of the day, the seed and the herbarium, and the three new forms with their faces. The 650 ms spark may be too short on the watch. They compile with hvigor from the command line: `LegacyCompileLiteJS` and `LegacyGenerateLiteCode` pass, and the build stops at `LegacySignLiteBin` as before. The unsigned `.bin` is 1.79 MB: the build stores images uncompressed, so a 180 px form takes about 130 KB however simple its PNG, and a herbarium image about 26 KB. This round added about 580 KB that way.

The rules were checked in Node (November 2026): saving, night, growth to each adult form, withering to a new seed, the clock going backwards, refusals and tap zones. The app compiles (`LegacyCompileLiteJS` and `LegacyGenerateLiteCode` pass). In the Previewer (November 2026): the main view and its layout, the seed's face in place, the water and clean buttons with their refusals, the status view, stroking, and the game starting, hiding the buttons and ending. Catching a spark, the sun button, night, bugs and the adult forms have not been seen there. There is no signed HAP yet: the build stops at `LegacySignLiteBin` until the debug profile `brotin.p7b` exists (`add-a-new-app.md`). What is left to check is in `watch-test-list.md`.
